## Scope and freshness

The daily refresh uses GitHub's public REST API without authentication. Only repository names, PR numbers, public links, states, and creation/merge dates are stored; titles, descriptions, comments, and private contribution totals are not collected into the snapshot.

Merged PRs use their merge date, not their creation date. Open PRs have not been merged. Closed PRs without a merge are counted separately. The timeline shows up to five recent merges.
